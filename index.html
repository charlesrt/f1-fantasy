Ways to drill in

Questions this season’s data can already answer.

  • Rewind the seasonPick any round above to see the table, podium and gaps as they stood that weekend.
  • Fair fightPoints per race entered levels the grid for teams who joined at Suzuka or Monaco.
  • Form guideLast-three-race average shows who is peaking now, not who banked points early.
  • ConsistencyA low spread means a steady scorer; a high one means boom-or-bust picks.
  • Purple sectorsCount who wins the most individual rounds — the weekend specialists.
  • Head-to-headFocus one team and compare its line to a rival’s on the cumulative chart.

    Accessibility statement

    This statement covers the aston martpx F1 Fantasy dashboard, its race snapshot, its race recap and its two animated films. We want everyone in the league to be able to follow the season, whatever the device, setting or assistive technology they use.

    How accessible this site is

    We aim for the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 at level AA, and we test against it. As far as we know, the site now meets that standard. The known limitations below each have an accessible alternative.

    What you can do

    • Use a keyboard alone. Every control can be reached with Tab and shows a clear yellow focus ring. Choose a round with the arrow keys, sort the timing tower, open and close the recap and films, and play, pause or skip through them (Space, and ← → to move 5 seconds).
    • Use a screen reader. Pages use headings, landmarks and labelled tables. Charts have text descriptions that point to the tables holding the same figures. The recap and films each come with a written summary, and Replay season announces each round as it plays.
    • Zoom to 400% or use a narrow screen. The layout reflows down to 320 pixels wide without scrolling sideways. Only the wide data tables scroll within their own box.
    • Turn motion down. If your device asks for reduced motion, charts and numbers change without animating. The recap and films open paused on their final frame, and you can still choose to play them.
    • Control sound. The only sound is a five-beep start-lights countdown in the grid race, under 3 seconds long. It plays only after you press play, and the Sound button turns it off.
    • Read without relying on colour. Text meets contrast of at least 4.5:1, controls at least 3:1. Meaning is never carried by colour alone: race bests carry ◆, scores above or below the median carry ▲ or ▼, and position changes carry arrows.

    Known limitations

    • The race snapshot is drawn as one picture so it can be shared as an image. Everything on it is also listed as text beside it.
    • The recap and film animations are drawn as pictures. Their on-screen text doesn’t respond to your own text-spacing settings. Their written summaries, and the tables on this page, give the same information as text.
    • Chart tooltips appear on mouse hover only (press Escape to close one). Every value in them is also in the timing tower or the race-by-race table.
    • Clicking a chart line or table row to focus a team needs a mouse. The Focus team menu in the season bar does the same from the keyboard.
    • The version of this dashboard inside Claude also depends on Claude’s own interface, which this statement doesn’t cover.

    How we tested it

    We last tested on 12 October 2026. We used automated checks (axe-core 4.10, WCAG 2.2 A and AA rules), checked colour contrast, and worked through every view with a keyboard alone, with reduced motion on, and at phone widths down to 320 pixels. We haven’t yet tested with a full range of screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA, JAWS) and will add that.

    Feedback and problems

    If anything stops you using the dashboard, or you need the data in another format, tell the league organiser in the league’s Slack channel. We’ll aim to reply within a week, and to fix problems before the next race weekend.

    This statement was prepared on 12 October 2026. We’ll review it after the final race of the season, or sooner if the site changes significantly.
